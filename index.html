Why attend

One Room. Many Perspectives.

Governments across Canada have made ambitious promises: net-zero targets, major infrastructure, sweeping transition plans. Turning promises into reality raises hard questions — about cost, capacity, timelines, and public buy-in.

The EMH Annual Forum 2026 brings modellers, social scientists, policymakers, utilities, and industry leaders into the same room to work through those questions, one at a time, together.

This EMH Annual Forum 2026 asks what it actually takes to get there — and whether we're asking the right questions.

Format

Diverse Perspectives, Timely Questions

Every panel is built around a tension: what the models say is possible, and what actually gets built. Hear directly from the modellers, regulators, policymakers, and industry leaders working to close that gap.

About the Award

EMH is accepting nominations for the 2026 Canadian Energy Modelling and Policymaking Hall of Fame.

Our field moves fast. New tools emerge, new questions appear, and the community keeps expanding. Yet behind this progress are individuals whose work has quietly and profoundly shaped how Canada plans its energy future. The Hall of Fame is about making those contributions visible.

This national recognition honours individuals whose work has advanced energy system modelling and evidence-based policymaking in Canada. It celebrates careers that strengthen analytical rigour, open up methods and data, and build the collaborative capacity the country relies on today.

The 2026 inductee will be recognized at the EMH Annual Forum 2026.

Nomination Criteria

Nominations are assessed against five criteria:

  • Impact on Canadian energy and climate policy
  • Advancement of modelling tools, methods, or standards
  • Leadership in collaboration across institutions and governments
  • Contribution to national modelling capacity and mentorship
  • Commitment to transparency, analytical rigour, and evidence-based policymaking

Note that the nominee must be present to accept the award at the EMH Annual Forum 2026 on December 16, 2026 in Ottawa, ON.

How to Nominate

Anyone in the Canadian energy community can submit a nomination, on behalf of an individual or an organization. The form takes about five minutes to complete.

A selection committee of modellers, policy practitioners, and researchers will review all submissions.

  • Nominations close: October 15, 2026
  • Finalists announced: November 2026
  • Recipients announced: December 16, 2026 at the EMH Annual Forum 2026

Past Inductee

2025 Hall of Fame Inductee: Nick Macaluso

Over nearly four decades in public service, including 25 years at Environment and Climate Change Canada, Nick Macaluso helped build core energy, economic, and emissions modelling capacity still used today. His work supported major national decisions, including carbon pricing, and extended internationally through long-standing contributions to UNFCCC transparency and review processes.
